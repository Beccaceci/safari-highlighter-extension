🚀 How to Test the Extension

  1. Return from Navbar to Square: Whenever you load or search a page, the annotator starts as a discreet little square (🖍️) docked in the top-right corner. Click it to enlarge into the full navbar. To return from the navbar back to the square, click the 🔲 button on the left of the dock header, or the 🗗 / ✕ buttons on the right, or simply press Escape!
  2. Forms & Polygons Popover: Click the 🔲▾ forms button on the dock to open the selection popover. Choose between Rectangle, Circle, Triangle, Diamond, Pentagon, Hexagon, Star, Arrow, Line, and Custom Polygon! With Custom Polygon, click anywhere on the page to place vertices—a rubber-band line tracks your cursor; click the starting green vertex (or double-click) to close the shape!
  3. Completely Clear Whole Page (Icon + Shortcut): Click the 🗑️ button in the actions group or press Ctrl+Shift+X (Cmd+Shift+X on Mac). This completely clears all canvas drawings, all highlighted text passages, and erases stored data for this URL.
  4. Vertical / Horizontal Navbar & Drag Handle: Click the ⇅ button to switch orientation. Grab the ⋮⋮ drag handle to smoothly move the navbar anywhere across the screen without sticky anchoring.
  5. Highlighter Cursor & Text Feedback: Select 🖍️ Highlight mode. The cursor matches your selected color and text blocks show subtle feedback. Selecting text highlights it cleanly without shifting line heights.
  6. Dual-Action Eraser (Drawings + Highlights): Switch to 🧹 Eraser to delete both vector drawings and highlighted text on touch.
  7. Undo & Redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y): Press Ctrl+Z (or Cmd+Z) to undo, and Ctrl+Y (or Cmd+Y / Ctrl+Shift+Z) to redo across drawings, shapes, and highlights!
  8. Laser Pointer (🔦): Click the 🔦 Laser button on the dock. Moving the cursor shows a pulsing glowing laser dot, and dragging creates a brilliant glowing neon laser beam that automatically fades away after ~1.2 seconds—ideal for presentations, tutorials, and pointing things out without leaving permanent marks!
  9. Save Annotations & Snapshot Export (Shortcuts): Press Cmd+S (or Ctrl+S / Alt+Shift+S) to instantly save all drawings and highlights for this URL. Press Cmd+Shift+E (or Ctrl+Shift+E) to export a PNG snapshot of the annotated page.

Section 1: Interactive Reading & Multi-Node Selection

In modern web browsers, annotating digital text should be as natural and frictionless as highlighting a physical book. When users review research papers, academic studies, or technical documentation, having persistent highlights across page visits is an essential productivity feature.

"The ability to annotate, summarize, and draw visual connections directly onto reading material dramatically increases information retention and conceptual synthesis."

Try selecting text that crosses formatting boundaries, such as bold words, italicized phrasing, or hyperlinked references. The highlighter handles boundary splitting and wraps nodes cleanly.

Section 2: Architecture & Key Concepts

Here are the core pillars of the Safari Web Annotator:

  • Shadow DOM Isolation: The toolbar dock resides in an isolated Shadow DOM root, guaranteeing zero style collisions with host pages.
  • Lossless Vector History: All drawing actions are stored as vector stroke models with quadratic Bézier curve smoothing rather than rasterized pixels.
  • Smart Stroke Eraser: The eraser detects collision paths with existing strokes, deleting entire targeted strokes without leaving pixel debris.
  • Local-First Storage: Highlights and explicitly saved drawings persist in browser.storage.local keyed to the page URL.
